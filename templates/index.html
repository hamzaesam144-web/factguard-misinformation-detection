Editorial Methodology

How Fact Guard works

The platform is designed to look serious, transparent, and close to a professional newsroom workflow.

01

Text Intake

The user submits a headline, paragraph, or full article for analysis.

02

Preprocessing

The text is cleaned and prepared for the fake-news detection model.

03

AI Classification

The model predicts whether the content is likely real, fake, or uncertain.

04

Confidence Report

The result is presented with a confidence score and simple explanation.
